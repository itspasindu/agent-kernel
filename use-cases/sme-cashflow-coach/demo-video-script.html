Scene 1 — Hook

0:00 – 0:15

Meet Pasindu. He runs a small bakery in Sri Lanka. Every day he sells buns, buys flour, and sometimes gives bread on customer credit. But his accounting system is still a notebook and memory. By the time he notices cash is tight, stock is already out — or rent is due.

On screen
  • Title card: SME Cashflow Coach
  • Quick B-roll: bakery / phone / WhatsApp icon

Scene 2 — Problem

0:15 – 0:45

Micro and small businesses don't need a heavy spreadsheet or a bank app. They need something simple, on WhatsApp, that turns daily messages into real numbers: balance, runway, stock, and who still owes money. That's what we built.

On screen
  • Text bullets: Late stock-outs · Rent shortfalls · Forgotten customer credit

Scene 3 — Solution

0:45 – 1:15

SME Cashflow Coach is a WhatsApp-first assistant built on Agent Kernel from Yaala Labs. Shop owners log sales and expenses in plain language. Four specialized agents work together — intake, cashflow analysis, business advice, and stock plus customer credit. All money math is deterministic in code, so the AI never invents balances.

On screen
  • Architecture: WhatsApp → Agent Kernel → domain.py → SQLite
  • Agents: ledger_desk · cashflow_analyst · business_advisor · stock_credit_desk

Scene 4 — LIVE: Menu

1:15 – 2:00

Let's see it live. Pasindu opens WhatsApp and messages the business bot. He types menu — and gets an interactive list: cash summary, dashboard, advice, trends, inventory, and customer credit. No forms. No training. Just tap what you need.

On screen — record live WhatsApp
  • Send: menu
  • Show interactive list opening
  • Tap Cash summary — show balance, runway, HEALTHY / TIGHT / CRITICAL

Scene 5 — LIVE: Log sale & credit

2:00 – 2:45

Now he logs today the way he already talks: "Sold twelve buns, twenty-four hundred." The system records the sale and updates cash. A customer, Amara, takes bread on credit. Pasindu sends: "Credit Amara five hundred bread." That's tracked separately — cash doesn't increase until she pays.

On screen — record live WhatsApp
  • Send: Sold 12 buns 2400
  • Wait for bot reply with updated balance
  • Send: credit Amara 500 bread
  • Optional: paid Amara 200

Scene 6 — LIVE: Insights

2:45 – 3:20

From the menu he checks inventory and customer credit. He asks what he should do next — and gets practical advice from the live ledger. If he asks, "Can I buy flour for three thousand?" — the coach checks affordability against runway, not guesswork. One message in. One clear answer out.

On screen — pick 2–3 live actions
  • Menu → Customer credit or Inventory
  • Menu → What should I do?
  • Or send: Can I buy flour for 3000?

Scene 7 — Agent Kernel & fallback

3:20 – 3:45

This project lives in the Agent Kernel repository under use-cases slash sme-cashflow-coach. It uses Agent Kernel for WhatsApp webhooks, session memory, multi-agent routing, and Gemini via Google ADK. Judges can also run desk.py with no API key — same SQLite ledger — to verify the math offline.

On screen (optional 10 sec cutaway)
  • Quick terminal: python desk.py dashboard
  • Or README / GitHub folder

Scene 8 — Close

3:45 – 4:00

SME Cashflow Coach gives micro-SME owners a daily cash discipline tool on the app they already use — WhatsApp. Built for SDG 8 and SDG 9. Not a bank. Not tax advice. Just honest numbers and better decisions. Thank you.
